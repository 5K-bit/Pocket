Pocket is a static installable web app (PWA): an iPhone Home Screen launchpad for OBEOS sites, repositories and dashboards. No server, account or build step; destination edits stay in the browser's local storage.

**Pre-release.** Desktop-browser verified; iPhone verification is a manual step (see `docs/runbook.md`).

### Verified
- Syntax and static deploy validation pass in CI.
- 18/18 headless-Chromium checks: tiles are https-only, credential-free, `noopener noreferrer`; service worker installs and caches the shell; the app launches offline; edits persist; `http:`, credential-bearing and `javascript:` URLs are rejected; restore-defaults works; after a simulated redeploy the new content and cache replace the old, online and offline. Transcript: `docs/evidence/pocket-e2e.txt`.
- The Netlify project `obeos-pocket` has a current, ready deploy.

### Known limitations
- Not tested on iOS Safari or an installed Home Screen app. Not tested against the live Netlify URL.
- Service worker registers only on HTTPS.
- Destinations are not encrypted, backed up or synced. Linked services are not available offline.
- No service health, authentication or notifications.

Files: `obeos-pocket-v0.1.1.zip` (runtime files only, deployable as a static site) and `SHA256SUMS`.
